Dance Centre

▼
REHEARSAL
Mon Jun 29 · 7:30 AM · Disney's Coronado Springs Resort. Bus Letter M departs resort 7:30 AM. Wear dance attire + Red Dance Shoes (socks suggested, tights not required). Bring All Access Pass — Park Hopper NOT needed. One parent meets at bus pick-up ~11:00 AM.
MK PARADE
Mon Jun 29 · 2:15 PM · Magic Kingdom. Performers meet as a group 1:25 PM at Carousel of Progress (side exit, Tomorrowland). Arrive with hair/makeup ready, in costume with a jacket/cover-up — don't change in public restrooms. Hair in a low ponytail, part right/left/center, rhinestone barrette on the right side, stage makeup with red lipstick, no jewelry, neutral nail polish only. Park Hopper Ticket required for performers and family. Bring a change of clothes for after in your Featured Performer bag. After the parade, performers change into street clothes and are escorted to Carousel of Progress for parent pickup.
